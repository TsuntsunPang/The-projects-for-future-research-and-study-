## The structure of the integrable book
### 1. Conventional Bethe Ansatz method to spin system (XXX model, XXY,XXZ model) and its higher spin generalization and long range interaction generalization (such as the Haldane-Shastry model)
### 2. The Bethe Ansatz to quantum gas which including the spin-0 Boson and spin-1/2 Fermion gas with the contact interaction. And its generalization to higher spin.
### 3. The Bethe Ansatz to strongly correlated system such as the integrable BCS model(Gaudin magnets), Hubbard model and Kondo model.The Kondo model including the conventional Kondo model and Anderson model. And it generalization to multichannel Kondo and topological Kondo.
### 4. The Bethe Ansatz to field theory such as the Sine Gordon model.
